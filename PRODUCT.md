# PRODUCT

**User.** Aspiring investors, scouts, operators, founders and startup fans who want to test their judgment, and teachers of entrepreneurship classes who want a quick exercise.

**Painful job.** Investment judgment has years-long feedback loops. You can't find out whether your instincts are any good without waiting for outcomes, and post-hoc stories make every winner look obvious.

**Promise.** In two minutes a day:
- judge five real pitches blind;
- see the sourced outcome;
- learn how often famous, well-funded ideas busted and dismissed ones broke out.

**Wedge.**
- A Wordle-style daily: same five for everyone, with a shareable emoji grid.
- Case-file pages that answer "what happened to <company>" searches.
- "You vs the TV investors" on show pitches.

**Why now.**
- Daily puzzle formats spread through share grids without paid acquisition.
- Two decades of startup outcomes are now public and well documented.
- Interest in "how VCs think" has never been higher.

**Activation metric.** `game_complete`: a visitor finishes a daily or practice round. Secondary: `share` and `call`. All are instrumented in `site/assets/*.js` and count once `PJ_GOATCOUNTER` is set.

**First revenue event.** A sponsor or GitHub Sponsors supporter via `PJ_SPONSOR_URL`. Later, off-Pages: a "pro" deck for classrooms, or an investor-scout program for top players. That needs accounts, so it is a later key.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- Manage money or "run a fund" from player scores (Investment Advisers Act and securities rules).
- Use recorded pitch videos without founder consent.
- Collect personal data or show a global leaderboard (no server).
- Present outcomes as advice about current companies.
